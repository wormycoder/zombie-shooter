FIX - LOOK INTO HOW MANY CORES OR THREADS THE GAME IS USING, MAKE SURE IT
      PRIORITIZES BEING AS EFFICIENT AS POSSIBLE - AND ALSO LOOK INTO GPU
      USAGE. DO NOTE, THIS GAME SHOULD BY DEFAULT NOT TAKE OVER 1GB OF RAM,
      ALTHOUGH THERE SHOULD BE A SETTING IN EXPERIMENTAL FOR THAT. DESIGN FOR
      4-8GB OF SYS MEM (2-3GB FREE), AIM FOR 16GB + OF SYS MEM (8GB+ FREE).
  -> HIGH. DONE (000.017.000.120).
     Measured (Chromium): the game's tab grew to ~800 MB touring a town, of
     which ~280 MB were kept pictures (walls and their darkened twins most of
     it) - the world data is only a few MB. A leak was found: the renderer's
     per-wall and per-object memos kept every picture evicted from the caches
     alive, and the caches painted duplicates. Fixed: ~700 MB now.
     New: Settings > Experimental > Memory budget (512 MB, 1 GB default,
     2/4/8 GB). Over it, pictures not drawn lately are dropped (repainted
     when needed) and the caches shrink until it fits. At 1 GB the game's tab
     and the browser's GPU process stay under ~0.9 GB; at 512 MB the tab
     stays ~460-550 MB. Shown in the detailed FPS counter (Settings > Display
     > Frame rate counter: Detailed) and the performance report.
     Threads/GPU: the game itself runs on one thread (JavaScript); the
     browser adds its own (compositor, raster, GPU process). Menus, the pause
     screen and a paused game now draw at 30 fps at most, so the processor
     and graphics card idle there. Still possible later: making the new
     world (about 1 s on Expanded) in a background worker.
